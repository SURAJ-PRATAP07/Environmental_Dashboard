# 🌍 Energy & Environmental Data Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing environmental, demographic, and energy indicators across India and global economies.

The project combines multi-source datasets to identify regional patterns, compare countries, and communicate key environmental and energy trends through interactive visualizations.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

The objective of this project was to transform multiple environmental, demographic, and energy datasets into an interactive analytical dashboard.

The analysis covers:

- Environmental indicators across **29 Indian states**
- Environmental and energy indicators across **190+ countries**
- Air Quality Index (AQI)
- Rainfall and groundwater availability
- Energy-source contributions
- Fossil-fuel consumption
- Carbon emissions
- Population trends
- Regional and country-level comparisons

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and interactive visualizations
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning, structuring, and consolidation
- **Power Query** – Data preparation and transformation
- **Data Visualization** – KPI Cards, Maps, Heat Maps, Histograms, and comparative visualizations
